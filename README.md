# AI Learning Experience Graph

> An AI-native system that turns genuine learning interactions into a living representation of how a person's knowledge and experience develops over time.

## 🚧 Project Status

**Early-stage / Pre-development**

This repository is currently being used to define the product concept, research the technical approach, and coordinate development for our AI-native product project.

---

## The Problem

People are increasingly using AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini to learn.

However, their learning progress remains largely invisible.

A person might spend months learning psychology, programming, mathematics, photography, or another subject through AI conversations, but there is no continuous representation of how their understanding has developed.

Traditional measures such as certificates, exams, and portfolios capture snapshots of ability. They do not necessarily capture the learning journey itself.

We want to explore whether AI can recognize that journey.

---

## The Idea

The **AI Learning Experience Graph** connects with the AI platforms people already use and observes their natural learning interactions.

Users choose the domains they want to learn and track.

As they interact with AI normally, the system identifies **learning signals** from their conversations and builds a longitudinal model of their progression.

The system then represents that progression through an **Experience Graph**.
